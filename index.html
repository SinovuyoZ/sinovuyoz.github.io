05 / Planet League · Fractional Growth / Paid Social · 2026

North East Nurdle Derby.

A community initiative with Northumberland Wildlife Trust and Newcastle United Foundation, turning beach walks into goals for supporters’ clubs. The programme is free to join: choose your club, take action and score goals. Prizes included club shirts, a Barbour voucher and a match ticket.

My role: creative variants, £3/day A/B test setup, landing-page tracking, performance reporting and onboarding review

Commercial questionWhich message gives supporters a reason to join, and where does that interest lose momentum before onboarding is complete?

What I built

  • A social rollout connecting club identity, beach-clean activity and clear participation steps.
  • A prize-hook versus comeback-hook test at £3 per variant per day, with separate tracking links.
  • Campaign reporting across traffic, landing-page views and engagement, alongside a separate onboarding snapshot.
Creative variations

“There’s more to play for this…” · “PRIZE ANNOUNCEMENT NEXT…” · “Round One is done!” · “GET THE KIDS OUT. MAKE A DAY OF IT.” · “Your next beach walk could score for your club”.
