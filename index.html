My Approach

Start with the customer.
Connect the commercial decisions.

Select an area to see the question I bring into a growth review.

Select any area above
I start with the audience, the intended action and the evidence available. Then I connect campaign, product and commercial decisions around the part of the journey that needs attention.
Customer behaviour
What is the person trying to achieve, and what are they actually doing? I use customer insight and observed behaviour to make the message and next action more relevant.
Audience and offer
Is the right audience encountering a proposition they can understand? Channel, positioning and creative should make the next step clear.
Progress through the journey
Where do interested people lose momentum? I review sign-up, onboarding and activation together so the campaign promise and product experience line up.
Commercial performance
What happens after the click or registration? Budget decisions need to reflect activation, funding or sales, alongside the cost of acquiring the response.
Partner contribution
What does a partner need to explain the offer and bring relevant customers? Communication, incentives and sales enablement need to support the same commercial objective.
Delivery and accountability
Who needs to make a decision or deliver the next step? I make priorities, briefs, approvals and reporting clear across commercial, marketing, product and creative teams.
